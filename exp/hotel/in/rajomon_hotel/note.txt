This experiment the Rajomon (admission control) with different types of scheduler including fifo, tailclipper, and our priority scheduler.
As Rajomon use the queue latency as signal, lower price up step or token replenish step will lead to conservative admission decision.
With our current configuration from 50 iterations of bayesian optimization, we conclude that scheduler will not cause major improvment in the goodput.
This is because the queue does not build and system is under-utlized.
